Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Link2QrCrafter is built with a privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information or QR code content you enter.

1. Zero Data Collection

All links, text, and customization settings you input are processed exclusively inside your web browser's memory. Nothing is ever sent to a backend server or third-party analytics service.

2. Local Storage

We only use your browser's localStorage to remember your Light/Dark theme preference. No tracking cookies or advertising identifiers are used.

3. Third-Party Libraries

This application loads styling (Tailwind CSS), fonts (Inter), and the QR generation library (qr-code-styling) from public CDNs. These static assets do not receive any of your input data.

Data & Security

How Link2QrCrafter keeps your data safe

100% Client-Side Processing: Every QR code is generated locally on your device using JavaScript. Because there is no server-side processing, your URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, or private notes never leave your browser.

Zero Server Uploads

When you click Download PNG or Copy Image, the image is exported directly from the HTML5 Canvas in your browser to your device's downloads folder or clipboard.

Offline-Ready Logic

Once the page is loaded, the QR generation engine operates completely independently without requiring network requests for encoding.
